About the Davis Open

The largest badminton tournament in Northern California.

Over 400 entries across 20 events, hosted in the University Credit Union Center. A stadium environment with 13 courts, professional lighting and sound. Organised entirely by UC Davis students.

Davis-themed trophies, cash prizes and tournament shirts await the winners.
