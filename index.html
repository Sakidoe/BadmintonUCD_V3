Who we are

A club for every UC Davis student who loves badminton.

We are a student-run sports club affiliated with the UC Davis Sport Club Program. We host weekly practices, travel across the US for Badminton tournaments, and organize as many events as possible for our members

We foster a welcoming and inclusive environment for all students stemming from a single shared interest, allowing players to compete together and manage events together, while also building life long friends through memories outside of the sport.
