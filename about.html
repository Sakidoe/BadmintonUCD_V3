Competing

From Hickey Gym to Collegiate Nationals.

We represent Northern California at the USA Badminton Collegiate Championships, and in 2026 the team brought home a 3rd-place national finish. Along the way we host meets against SJSU, UCSC, Stanford and UC Berkeley, and take an annual road trip to the Irvine collegiate tournament.
