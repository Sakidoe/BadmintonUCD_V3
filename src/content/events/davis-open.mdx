## About the Davis Open

Our student-run tournament brings 400+ entries across 20 events to the University Credit Union Center...13 courts, stadium lighting and sound, trophies and cash prizes.

We run it twice a year: the **Davis Fall Open** and the **Davis Spring Open**.
